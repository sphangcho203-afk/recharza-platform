## 1. Terms of Service

### 1.1 Platform purpose
Recharza provides access to digital game top-ups, virtual currency, passes, memberships, vouchers, and related digital products supplied or fulfilled through approved third-party providers. Product availability, pricing, regions, processing times, and fulfilment methods may differ by game and supplier market.

### 1.2 Customer eligibility
Customers must provide accurate information and must be legally permitted to use the selected payment method and game account. A parent or guardian should supervise purchases made by minors where required by law or the relevant game publisher.

### 1.3 Game-account details
The customer is responsible for entering the correct player ID, UID, Riot ID, zone ID, server, region, or other destination information. Recharza may validate the format or nickname where supported, but the customer must complete the final review before payment.

### 1.4 Prices and payment
The final payable amount shown at checkout controls the transaction. Currency conversions displayed before payment may be estimates unless explicitly locked by the payment session. A purchase is not confirmed until Recharza records successful payment or another approved payment state.

### 1.5 Digital fulfilment
Digital products may begin processing immediately after payment confirmation. Once a top-up, code, pass, membership, or virtual currency has been successfully delivered to the selected destination, it may be irreversible.

### 1.6 Prohibited activity
Customers must not use Recharza for fraud, stolen payment instruments, money laundering, account abuse, unauthorized resale, chargeback manipulation, attempts to bypass regional restrictions, or activity prohibited by a game publisher or supplier.

### 1.7 Account security
Customers are responsible for protecting passwords, tracking credentials, email accounts, and devices. Recharza support will never request a password, OTP, UPI PIN, card PIN, or remote-device access.

### 1.8 Availability and changes
Recharza may suspend a product, region, payment method, account, or order where security, supplier availability, compliance, or fulfilment integrity requires it. Material changes to these Terms should be reflected by an updated effective date.

### 1.9 Publisher relationship
Game names, logos, currencies, characters, and artwork belong to their respective owners. Recharza is an independent digital top-up platform unless a specific official relationship is clearly stated.

### 1.10 Contact
Questions about these Terms may be sent to `recherzatopup@gmail.com`.

## 2. Privacy Policy

### 2.1 Information collected
Recharza may collect account details, contact information, game-account identifiers, billing address information, payment references, order history, support communications, device/browser information, security events, and limited operational logs.

### 2.2 Payment information
Sensitive payment credentials are processed by approved payment providers. Recharza should not store complete card numbers, CVV values, UPI PINs, card PINs, or OTPs.

### 2.3 How information is used
Information may be used to create accounts, validate destinations, process payments, fulfil orders, provide receipts, prevent fraud, secure the platform, recover accounts, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, and improve customer experience.

### 2.4 Service providers
Necessary information may be shared with payment providers, game-product suppliers, email providers, hosting/database providers, fraud-prevention services, and professional advisers under appropriate contractual or legal safeguards.

### 2.5 Retention
Information should be retained only for the period required for fulfilment, account operation, security, tax/accounting, dispute handling, supplier reconciliation, and legal compliance. Retention periods must be finalized during legal review.

### 2.6 Customer choices
Customers may request access, correction, or deletion where applicable. Some records may need to be retained for legal, fraud-prevention, accounting, or dispute-resolution purposes.

### 2.7 Security
Recharza uses access controls, encrypted connections, protected environment variables, limited operational permissions, and audit records. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

### 2.8 Children and minors
Recharza does not knowingly seek unnecessary personal information from children. Purchases by minors should be supervised where required.

### 2.9 Contact
Privacy requests may be sent to `recherzatopup@gmail.com`.

## 3. Refund and Cancellation Policy

### 3.1 Before fulfilment
A cancellation or refund may be considered when payment has been captured but fulfilment has not started, subject to payment-provider and supplier restrictions.

### 3.2 Successful digital delivery
A successfully delivered digital top-up, code, pass, membership, or virtual currency is generally non-refundable because it cannot reliably be recovered or transferred.

### 3.3 Eligible refund situations
A refund or replacement may be considered where:

- payment was captured more than once for the same protected order;
- Recharza confirms that fulfilment permanently failed;
- the supplier confirms non-delivery;
- Recharza delivered a different product from the confirmed order;
- the payment provider reversed or failed the transaction while Recharza did not fulfil it;
- applicable law requires another remedy.

### 3.4 Incorrect customer information
Orders sent to an incorrect player ID, UID, server, zone, region, or account because the customer submitted or approved incorrect information are generally not refundable after fulfilment.

### 3.5 Pending orders
A pending order is not automatically failed. Recharza may wait for payment-provider or supplier reconciliation before approving a refund.

### 3.6 Refund timing
Approved refunds are returned through the original payment route where possible. Bank or payment-provider processing time is outside Recharza's direct control.

### 3.7 Disputes
Customers should contact support with the order ID before raising a payment dispute so Recharza can investigate fulfilment and payment records.

## 5. Delivery and Fulfilment Policy

### 5.1 Delivery method
Products may be delivered directly to a game account, as a digital code, through a supplier fulfilment process, or through another method clearly shown before purchase.

### 5.2 Processing time
Estimated processing times are not guarantees. Delays may result from payment review, supplier maintenance, game maintenance, regional restrictions, invalid destination information, security checks, or unusual demand.

### 5.3 Order statuses
Typical statuses include order created, payment pending, payment confirmed, fulfilment processing, delivered, failed, cancelled, or refunded. The private order timeline is the primary status source.

### 5.4 Customer responsibility
The customer must keep the order ID and private tracking credential secure and must review the selected game, market, package, player destination, and payable amount before payment.
